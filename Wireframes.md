# Wireframes & Prototypes

## 1. Introduction

This document will house the wireframes and prototypes for the [Project Name]. Wireframes are basic visual guides that represent the skeletal framework of the application. They are a crucial step in the design process, allowing us to focus on layout, functionality, and user flow without being distracted by colors, fonts, or images.

## 2. Process

Our design process will follow these steps:

1.  **Low-Fidelity Wireframes (Discovery Phase):**
    *   Based on the requirements gathered, we will create simple, black-and-white sketches of the main screens.
    *   These will be used to facilitate discussions with stakeholders and ensure we are aligned on the core layout and functionality.
    *   **Tools:** Balsamiq, Sketch, or similar.

2.  **High-Fidelity Prototypes (Design Phase):**
    *   Once the low-fidelity wireframes are approved, we will create detailed, interactive prototypes.
    *   These will include UI elements, branding, and a more polished look and feel.
    *   The prototypes will be clickable, allowing stakeholders to experience the user flow as if they were using the live application.
    *   **Tools:** Figma, Adobe XD, or similar.
