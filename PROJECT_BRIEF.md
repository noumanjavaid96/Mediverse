# Mediverse Project Brief: Doctor-Centric Marketplace

## 1. Project Direction: Doctor-Centric Marketplace

The Mediverse platform will be developed as a **Doctor-Centric Marketplace**. The primary goal of this platform is to connect patients with doctors directly, allowing them to search for, book, and manage appointments in a seamless manner.

---

## 2. Core User Roles & Features

### 2.1. Patient

*   **Authentication:**
    *   Secure sign-up and login functionality.
*   **Search & Filtering:**
    *   Search for doctors by name, specialty, or medical condition.
    *   Filter results by location, insurance provider, availability, and patient ratings.
*   **Doctor Profiles:**
    *   View comprehensive doctor profiles, including credentials, experience, photos, and affiliated hospitals.
*   **Appointment Management:**
    *   Book, reschedule, or cancel appointments based on the doctor's real-time availability.
*   **Patient Dashboard:**
    *   A centralized dashboard to view upcoming and past appointments.
    *   Access to session notes and e-prescriptions shared by the doctor.
    *   Manage personal, contact, and insurance information.
*   **Feedback System:**
    *   Ability to leave reviews and ratings for doctors after a completed consultation.

### 2.2. Doctor

*   **Authentication:**
    *   Secure login for registered doctors.
*   **Profile Management:**
    *   Create and manage a detailed professional profile, including specialties, experience, and hospital affiliations.
*   **Schedule Management:**
    *   Set and update their schedule and availability for patient appointments.
*   **Appointment Management:**
    *   View and manage their calendar of upcoming appointments.
    *   Ability to approve or reject appointment requests.
*   **Patient Interaction (Future Scope):**
    *   Functionality for conducting telehealth sessions.
    *   Tools for writing and sharing session notes and e-prescriptions.

### 2.3. Super Admin

*   **Authentication:**
    *   Secure login to the admin portal.
*   **Doctor Management:**
    *   Onboard new doctors and verify their credentials.
    *   Manage the directory of approved doctors on the platform.
*   **Platform Oversight:**
    *   Manage platform-wide data, such as medical specialties, approved insurance providers, etc.
*   **Reporting & Analytics:**
    *   Access to a dashboard with key platform metrics, including booking volumes, popular specialties, and user growth.

---

## 3. High-Level Patient Journey

The patient's interaction with the platform will follow a clear and intuitive path:

1.  **Search:** The patient uses the search and filter functions to find a doctor that meets their needs.
2.  **Discover:** The patient reviews detailed doctor profiles to make an informed decision.
3.  **Book:** The patient selects an available time slot and books their appointment.
4.  **Manage:** The patient uses their personal dashboard to manage their appointments, view their health records, and update their profile.
